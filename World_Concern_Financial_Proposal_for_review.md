6. Pricing Assumptions and Dependencies
The estimates assume:
Approximately 50 employees across five offices.
One Kenyan employing/payroll entity.
One initial migration using validated spreadsheet or CSV data.
One HR data exchange and one accounting data exchange, using documented interfaces or approved structured-file exchange.
Timely access to relevant source data, payroll policies, system specifications, reviewers and test environments.
World Concern's review and approval of statutory sources, payroll rules, security requirements, integration mappings and acceptance criteria.
Delivery within the agreed scope and implementation plan.
The final scope and price depend on discovery, validation of payroll and statutory requirements, data quality, external-system access, provider quotations and written agreement on deliverables and acceptance conditions.
Changes to the assumptions or scope may affect price and schedule. Such changes will be assessed and agreed in writing before the additional work is undertaken.
