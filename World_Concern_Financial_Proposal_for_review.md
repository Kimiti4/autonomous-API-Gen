## 3. Implementation payment milestones

Each milestone is a percentage of the **KES 1,310,000 implementation fee only**. Recurring hosting and support are excluded from these milestone amounts and must not be charged again through them.

| Implementation milestone | Share of implementation | Amount (KES) | Payment trigger |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Contract signing and approved project kickoff | 20% | 262,000 | Signed contract, approved project charter and kickoff |
| Requirements and solution design approval | 20% | 262,000 | Signed traceability matrix, requirements/design baseline and acceptance plan |
| Core payroll modules ready for testing | 25% | 327,500 | Agreed scope modules available in test environment with delivery evidence |
| UAT and parallel-run acceptance | 25% | 327,500 | Signed UAT and two consecutive parallel-run reconciliation results |
| Deployment and handover | 10% | 131,000 | Approved production release (if go-live gates pass), training and handover sign-off |
| **Implementation total** | **100%** | **1,310,000** | |

If statutory, security, UAT or parallel-run acceptance is not met, the relevant milestone is not deemed accepted merely because a calendar date has passed. The contract should define how a delayed or failed acceptance is handled.

## 5. Licensing, tax and third-party costs

- **Separate software licence:** KES 0 is the provisional estimate for a separate Tiannara ESAP licence charge for the initially agreed project scope only. This is not a statement that source-code ownership, perpetual rights, transfer rights or reuse rights are included. Those rights and any licence must be expressly agreed in the contract before work starts.
- **VAT and taxes:** The bidder's VAT/tax status and applicable tax treatment are unconfirmed. Amounts are exclusive of any applicable taxes; no VAT PIN or tax registration is claimed. Confirm treatment and show any applicable tax separately before signature.
- **Hosting:** KES 10,000/month is a planning assumption, not a cloud-provider quotation, secured contract, reserved region or data-residency guarantee. Kenya/East Africa location is preferred only if a provider meets approved security, privacy, availability and recovery requirements.
- **Excluded third-party charges:** SMS/email consumption, premium hosting, bank/payment services, existing HR/accounting vendor API fees, additional licences, data migration beyond scope and non-standard interfaces are not priced. Identify and approve such charges before commitment.
- **Budget ceiling:** Unknown. KES 1,670,000 is an indicative first-year estimate, not the buyer's ceiling or an authorized final offer.

## 6. Pricing assumptions

Approximately 50 employees; five offices; one Kenyan employing/payroll entity; one initial validated spreadsheet/CSV migration; one HR and one accounting integration or approved structured-file exchange; timely access to reviewers, source data, system specifications and test environments. Statutory rules, security requirements, interface availability, provider pricing and final scope require discovery and written approval.
