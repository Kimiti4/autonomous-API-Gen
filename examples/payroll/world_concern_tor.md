# World Concern Development Organization — Payroll Software TOR

## BACKGROUND

World Concern Development Organization is a Christian global relief and development agency serving communities facing extreme poverty and disaster vulnerability. The regional office is in Nairobi, Kenya. The stated operating profile is approximately 50 employees across five offices. Employment types include contract employees, short-term contract employees, and interns.

## OBJECTIVES

- Automate payroll calculations and processing.
- Ensure compliance with applicable tax laws and statutory deductions.
- Reduce payroll processing time and human errors.
- Generate accurate payroll reports and payslips.
- Integrate with existing HR and accounting systems.
- Maintain secure employee payroll records.
- Provide audit trails and management reporting.

## EMPLOYEE INFORMATION MANAGEMENT

- Maintain employee master records.
- Track employment history.
- Manage departments and positions.
- Manage employee bank details.
- Manage employee documents.

## PAYROLL PROCESSING

- Calculate salaries.
- Calculate overtime.
- Manage allowances and benefits.
- Process bonuses and incentives.
- Apply loan and salary advance deductions.
- Apply leave deductions.
- Calculate arrears and back pay.
- Support monthly, bi-weekly, and weekly pay frequencies.
- Allocate payroll costs.

## STATUTORY COMPLIANCE

- Calculate income tax.
- Calculate social security contributions.
- Calculate pension deductions.
- Calculate health insurance deductions.
- Support other statutory deductions applicable under local legislation.
- Generate statutory returns and reports.

The TOR does not provide statutory rates, tax bands, effective dates, rounding rules, treatment of taxable benefits, or authoritative source versions. These must be clarified and approved before statutory payroll calculations can be certified.

## PAYSLIP MANAGEMENT

- Generate electronic payslips.
- Distribute payslips by email.
- Provide employee self-service access to payslips.
- Retrieve historical payslips.

## REPORTING

- Generate payroll summaries.
- Generate departmental payroll reports.
- Generate tax reports.
- Generate deduction reports.
- Generate bank transfer schedules.
- Generate audit reports.
- Support custom management reports.

## EMPLOYEE SELF SERVICE

- Allow employees to view payslips.
- Allow employees to update personal information subject to approval.
- Allow employees to view leave balances.
- Allow employees to apply for leave.
- Allow employees to download payroll-related documents.

## TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS

- Provide a web-based and/or cloud-hosted system.
- Support role-based access controls.
- Support multi-user access.
- Support data backup and recovery.
- Include audit trail functionality.
- Support major web browsers.
- Support mobile access where applicable.
- Import and export common formats including Excel, CSV, and PDF.
- Allocate an individual staff member's salary and benefits across multiple cost centers.

## SECURITY REQUIREMENTS

The supplied TOR contains a security heading but does not enumerate specific security controls. World Concern must confirm authentication, authorization, data encryption, retention, incident response, backup protection, privileged access, and audit-log requirements before security acceptance.

## SUPPORT AND MAINTENANCE

- Provide helpdesk support.
- Provide system updates and upgrades.
- Provide a service-level agreement.
- Conduct periodic performance reviews.

## BUDGET AND PAYMENT TERMS

- Specify software licensing costs.
- Specify implementation costs.
- Specify training costs.
- Specify annual maintenance fees.
- Disclose additional recurring charges.

## ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

- Complete agreed testing successfully.
- Demonstrate accurate payroll processing against approved test cases.
- Demonstrate compliance with approved statutory requirements.
- Demonstrate successful integration with existing systems.
- Deliver agreed documentation and training.
- Obtain sign-off by authorized management.
